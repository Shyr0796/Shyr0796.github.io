01

About

My work sits at the intersection of physics-based simulation, artificial intelligence, and urban safety.

I am interested in simulation systems that are not only fast or visually convincing, but also testable, auditable, and useful for real decisions. My current research spans multi-agent systems, disaster simulation, evacuation navigation, crowd dynamics, and digital-twin scenario deduction.

Before joining HKUST(GZ), I received an M.Eng. in Structural Engineering from the Institute of Engineering Mechanics, China Earthquake Administration, and a B.E. in Civil Engineering from Beijing Jiaotong University.

Core research question

How can we develop generalizable, verifiable simulations and effectively integrate AI to enable prediction, optimization, and decision-making?

02

Research focus

01 / Verification

Generalizable simulation

Reproducible models with explicit assumptions, measurable behavior, and evidence that separates engineering functionality from scientific validity.

02 / Intelligence

AI + simulation

Integrating learning and multi-agent reasoning with deterministic simulators for prediction, optimization, diagnosis, and decision support.

03 / Urban safety

Disaster & crowd dynamics

Modeling fire, evacuation, density-hazard coupling, and collective movement in dense and informal urban environments.
